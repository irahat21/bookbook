BookOrbit AI is an online bookstore with digital books. There is no shopping cart: each book is bought on its own with the Buy Now button and paid from the customer's wallet balance. Books are digital, so there is no stock limit, and a customer can buy each book only once.

Visitors can browse the catalog, search by title, author, keyword or publication year, search by a photo of a cover, read reviews and use the AI chatbot for store questions. To buy books, write reviews or use the Reading Companion, a visitor registers as a customer.

Customers and Super-Users sign in with a face sample, or with a voice sample as the alternative. A face must match with a similarity of at least 0.60 and a voice with at least 0.75. After three failed sign-in attempts in a row the username is locked for five minutes.

The wallet holds the customer's money. Customers add funds with a deposit of up to $10,000 at a time. Every deposit, purchase and fine appears in the ledger on the My Balance page. The prototype uses fake money only; there is no payment gateway.

VIP status: a Registered Customer becomes a VIP Customer right after a purchase that brings their purchases to more than 3, or their spending to more than $200, counted since joining or since their last demotion. VIP Customers pay 90 percent of the list price, a 10 percent discount rounded to the cent. The discount starts with the first purchase after the promotion.

Insufficient funds: if a customer tries to buy a book without enough money in the wallet, the purchase is rejected and a fine of $1.00 is charged. If the fine takes the balance below $0.00, the account is suspended.

Suspended accounts can only use the wallet, to deposit money and view the ledger. A Super-User can reopen a suspended account once its balance is $0.00 or more. Reopening resets the customer's warnings to 0.

Reviews: customers rate books from 1 to 5 stars and may add a comment. Each customer writes one review per book. Comments are checked for taboo words. A comment with 1 to 3 taboo words is published with those words replaced by asterisks. A comment with more than 3 taboo words is blocked and the author receives a warning.

Warnings: a Registered Customer whose warnings go above 2, that is the third warning, is suspended. A VIP Customer who receives a warning loses VIP status and becomes a Registered Customer again, and their promotion counters restart.

Ratings: each book shows a weighted average rating. Ratings from verified buyers, customers who bought the book, count twice as much as other ratings. Books with more than 3 ratings and an average below 2 go on the No-Order List; books with more than 3 ratings and an average above 4 go on the To-Order List.

Popular books are ranked by browse count plus three times the purchase count; the top 3 are shown on the home page. Signed-in customers instead see 3 recommended books based on the categories, authors and keywords of the books they bought and viewed.

The AI chatbot answers questions about the store for everyone. Registered and VIP Customers can also ask about their own account and ask general questions. Super-Users can also ask about administrative data such as suspended accounts, warnings, sales and the order lists.

The Reading Companion is available to Registered and VIP Customers for books that have a full text. It can summarize a chapter, explain a selected passage, define a word, quiz the reader and answer questions about the book. Owned books can be read in full; for other books only the first chapter is available.
